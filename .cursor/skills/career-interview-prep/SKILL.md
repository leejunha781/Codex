# Career & Interview Prep

## When to Use

- Mock interview questions
- STAR story development
- Role positioning (FAE, Technical PM, BDM, PM)
- 30–60 second elevator answers

## User Strengths to Emphasize

- 15 years ROK Navy programmes at Daeyang Electric — systems integration, stakeholder management
- Intellian LEO terminal — FAT/SAT, commissioning, customer-facing technical delivery
- Embedded systems education + field execution bridge
- PLM/traceability mindset from shipbuilding and defence

## Answer Structure (30–60 sec)

1. **Headline** — direct answer in one sentence
2. **Example** — one concrete project (Navy programme or Intellian commissioning)
3. **Result** — measurable or stakeholder outcome
4. **Link** — tie back to target role

## Tone Rules

- Calm, grateful, technically grounded
- Never desperate or negative about past employers
- Do not say "I do not know PLM" — frame as learning/growth if needed

## Common Themes

| Theme | Example Angle |
|-------|----------------|
| Technical depth | Embedded systems, integration, validation |
| Customer/stakeholder | Navy programmes, SAT/FAT with operators |
| Learning agility | LEO satellite new technology adoption |
| Traceability | PLM, requirements, delivery evidence |

## Output Format

- English for practice answers (interview language)
- Korean summary/explanation if user prefers
- Optional flashcard: Question → 3 bullet talking points
