## Post tone for freelance angles

- **Consultative, not salesy:** share how the work is done, what fails without structure, what evidence matters — do not list prices or "hire me" CTAs in every post.
- **Credibility through process:** intake → structure → draft → review → deliver; show engineering judgment, not raw AI output.
- **One concrete example per post:** e.g. satellite terminal link-loss RCA, LEO FAT/SAT checklist, AVEVA PLM concept deck outline.
- **Soft CTA only:** end with "If your team needs…" or "message me" at most once per month; most posts should be pure insight.
- **Hashtags:** mix domain tags (#MarinePLM #Shipbuilding #SATCOM) with freelance-relevant tags (#TechnicalWriting #EngineeringConsulting #RootCauseAnalysis) — 5–8 total.
