1. **JD or role target** — URL, pasted text, or role description
2. **Canonical resume** — Never overwrite original; copy to `_Revised_JD_Fit` or `_Targeted_YYYYMMDD`
3. **Fit analysis** — Score, evidence, gaps, strategy (Korean OK for analysis)
4. **Tailored resume + cover** — English formal; Intellian/Daeyang/defense/satellite aligned to JD
5. **QA** — PDF export + visual check via Office COM
