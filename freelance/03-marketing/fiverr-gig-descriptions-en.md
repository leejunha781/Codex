## GIG 1 — Engineering Reports, RCA & FAT/SAT Documents

### Gig title (80 characters max)

```
I will write engineering RCA reports, FAT SAT documents and technical proposals
```

*Alt:* `I will write professional engineering reports, RCA and FAT SAT documents`

### Category

Writing & Translation → Technical Writing

### Search tags

engineering report, RCA report, FAT SAT, technical writing, root cause analysis, technical proposal, troubleshooting report, system integration, defense, shipbuilding

### Gig description

```
Do you have the technical facts but need a professional document your customer, QA team or management can sign off on?

I am a freelance engineering documentation specialist with 21+ years in naval shipbuilding, defense electronics, satellite communications and system integration. I turn your field notes, test logs, photos and issue data into clear, structured English documents — not generic AI text.

WHAT I CAN WRITE FOR YOU

✔ Root Cause Analysis (RCA) reports
✔ FAT / SAT / HAT test procedures and checklists
✔ Customer-facing issue and trouble reports
✔ Technical proposal drafts (scope, WBS outline, risks)
✔ Installation and commissioning procedures
✔ Issue analysis and verification evidence summaries

TYPICAL INPUT I NEED FROM YOU

• Equipment name and model
• Problem description / symptoms
• Environment (site, vessel, rack, network)
• Test results and logs
• Root cause hypothesis (if known)
• Corrective actions and verification evidence

WHAT YOU GET

• Professional English document (Word + PDF)
• Clear structure: summary, evidence tables, root cause, corrective actions, verification
• Engineering judgment applied — I review and refine, not just auto-generate
• One revision round included

WHO THIS IS FOR

• Equipment manufacturers and system integrators
• Defense and shipbuilding suppliers
• Satellite / telecom field support teams
• SMEs that need customer-ready documents but lack dedicated technical writers

WHY WORK WITH ME

I have written and supported FAT/SAT, commissioning and customer technical communication in real marine and satellite programmes. I understand how field issues, power integrity, grounding, test evidence and customer expectations connect — and how to document them properly.

Message me before ordering with a short description of your issue or document type. I will confirm scope and timeline.

Portfolio samples available on request.
```

A: No. Send notes, logs, photos or a call summary. I work from your inputs.

**Q: Can you sign an NDA?**  
A: Yes. Customer and project details are kept confidential.

**Q: Is this only for satellite or marine equipment?**  
A: No. I specialise in complex systems (defense, marine, telecom, industrial automation) but any equipment with field test evidence is fine.

**Q: Do you use AI?**  
A: I use AI tools to speed up drafting, but every deliverable is reviewed and refined with engineering judgment before delivery.
