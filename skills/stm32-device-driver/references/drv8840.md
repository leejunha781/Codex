# TI DRV8840 integration

Use the official DRV8840 datasheet and the exact schematic revision as primary evidence. The DRV8840 is a single brushed-DC H-bridge with a parallel digital interface; it is not a UART servo controller.

## Confirmed device contract

- VM recommended operating range: 8.2 V to 45 V. Keep outputs disabled until board voltage, current limit, and motor rating are confirmed.
- Pin 21 `ENBL` enables the bridge and accepts PWM for speed/current chopping control. Pin 20 `PHASE` selects direction.
- Pin 18 `nFAULT` is open drain. Validate the board pull-up voltage and treat assertion as a safety event.
- Pins 16 `nRESET` and 17 `nSLEEP` have internal pulldowns and must be high for operation. Allow at least 1 ms after wake before driving.
- Pin 19 `DECAY`: low selects slow decay/brake during disabled PWM intervals; high selects fast decay/coast. Confirm the board strap or GPIO before defining stop behavior.
- Full-scale trip current is `I_TRIP = VREF / (5 * R_SENSE)`. Codes `I0..I4` select one of 32 current levels. Bound every calculation against the fitted sense resistor, VREF range, motor rating, thermal design, and supply limit.
- Overcurrent shutdown is latched until nRESET or VM power cycling. Thermal shutdown can resume after cooling; undervoltage lockout resets internal logic and resumes when VM recovers. Firmware must distinguish these recovery paths.

Also present are per-channel nRESET, nFAULT, ADC current-feedback, H-bridge outputs, and differential encoder nets. Determine exact MCU GPIO pins, timer alternate functions, DECAY/nSLEEP straps, current-code wiring, VREF source, sense-resistor value, fault pull-up, encoder receiver polarity, and motor limits from schematic visual verification and board measurement before implementation.

## Architecture boundary

Do not merge this driver into an unrelated MCU project solely because it is open in the workspace. The supplied PROC-D100 IO schematic and `PEC100_MCPU_V130.hex` describe an STM32F722 MCPU. A separate STM32F746 SCPU project can communicate with it through command/status signals without directly owning the DRV8840. Reconstruct the F722 firmware separately or prove the inter-processor protocol before changing SCPU motor commands.

Treat TI EVM packages containing `.exe`, `.dll`, or `.ocx` files as tools, not source examples, unless the archive also contains auditable source files. Never execute vendor binaries during artifact inspection.
